Our Culture

Collaborate. Create. Grow.

Three words, written on the wall — and applied in every code review, every hire, every customer call. They're how we decide who fits and how we work.

01 — Collaborate

Every voice in the room.

Collaboration is the default at FactWise. Regardless of seniority, every team member is expected to voice their perspective, push back, and shape the work.

02 — Create

Elegant solutions to hard problems.

We value the passion, dedication, and adaptability that lets us craft elegant, beautiful solutions for some of the most complex challenges in enterprise procurement.

03 — Grow

On your terms, at your pace.

We believe in striving for excellence, holding each other accountable, being transparent — and having fun while doing it. Every team member grows their own way.
