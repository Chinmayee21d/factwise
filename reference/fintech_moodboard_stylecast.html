07 — Brand Personality
Trustworthy
Clean lines, strong contrast, and data-backed claims. Never flashy for its own sake — every visual choice earns confidence.
Modern & Capable
Rounded corners, generous padding, and smooth gradients signal a product that's sophisticated without being cold or corporate.
Approachable
Human photography, soft background washes, and friendly CTAs lower the barrier to entry for all financial literacy levels.
Global & Inclusive
Country flags, multi-currency support, and a world-map CTA section reinforce that this platform belongs to everyone, everywhere.

Charts & graphs
Photography: Natural light, confident but friendly expressions, no stock-photo stiffness. Illustrations: Flat vector UI elements, abstract financial graphs, geometric world maps. Avoid: Generic office scenes, pure stock photography, realistic 3D renders.
11 — Design Principles
Clarity First
Every element earns its place. If it doesn't guide the user toward action, remove it.
Structured Space
Generous whitespace creates visual breathing room and signals premium quality.
Blue as Language
Primary blue is reserved for actions and trust cues — never decorative use only.
Mobile-Led
App-first flows shown prominently. Desktop is an extension, not the primary canvas.
Trust Signals
Stats, social proof, and security badges are woven through — not buried in footers.
Scalable Depth
Light, mid, and dark surface tiers create consistent hierarchy without heavy shadows.
