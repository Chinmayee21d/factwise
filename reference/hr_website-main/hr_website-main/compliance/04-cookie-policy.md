## 2. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over time.

We also use related technologies:

| Technology | What it does |
|---|---|
| Session storage | Temporary data held in browser memory — deleted when you close the tab |
| Local storage | Persistent data stored in your browser — survives tab and browser restarts |
| Pixels / web beacons | Tiny invisible images that confirm a page was loaded or an email was opened |
| Browser fingerprinting | We do NOT use this |

## 3. Categories of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary

Essential to operate the Platform. Without these, login, page navigation, and Magic Link assessments cannot work. These do not require your consent.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| hrops_session | Authenticates your login session | Session (cleared on logout) |

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|

| hrops_analytics_id | Anonymous session identifier | 12 months |

We do not use Google Analytics.

Track visits for advertising measurement and retargeting. Only placed if you explicitly opt in.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Set by |
|---|---|---|---|

## 7. Legal Basis

### DPDP Act 2023 (India)

Where cookies process personal data of Indian users, consent is:

- **Free** — no feature is withheld for refusing non-essential cookies
- **Specific** — separate consent requested per category
- **Informed** — this policy details what each cookie does
- **Unambiguous** — obtained through affirmative action (clicking Accept), not pre-ticked boxes

### GDPR (EU/EEA visitors)

| Category | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Strictly necessary | Legitimate interest / contract — no consent required |
| Functional | Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) |
| Analytics | Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) |
| Marketing | Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) |

Consent can be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
