## 4. Files Never Touch Our Servers

HR Ops is architected around **data minimisation**. Resumes, assessment documents, and interview recordings are stored in your own cloud storage (Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, or Dropbox/Box). HR Ops generates scoped, time-limited access links to those files rather than copying them to our infrastructure.

This means:
- We do not store your candidates' resume files on HR Ops servers
- We process the text content of resumes for AI screening but do not retain the underlying files
- Extracted text is retained only for the duration of the hiring process

## 5. AI Processing and Automated Decision-Making

### 5.1 What the AI does

HR Ops uses AI to:
- Screen resumes against job descriptions and produce a score and verdict (Proceed / Maybe / Decline)
- Generate plain-English reasoning for each screening decision
- Flag panel variance (when interviewers' scores diverge significantly)
- Provide natural language analytics (employers querying hiring data in plain English)

### 5.2 Human review requirement

**AI screening verdicts are recommendations, not final decisions.** Every Proceed/Maybe/Decline verdict is presented to a human recruiter or hiring manager who makes the actual hiring decision. We do not make automated final decisions about candidates without human involvement.

This satisfies:
- **GDPR Article 22** â€” right not to be subject to solely automated decisions
- **DPDP Act** obligations on automated processing
- **EU AI Act** requirements for human oversight of high-risk AI systems (recruitment AI is classified as high-risk)

### 5.3 AI model training

We do not use personal data of candidates or employees processed through the Platform to train our core AI models unless:
- The customer has explicitly agreed in writing (via a separate model training addendum)
- The candidate has given separate, specific consent
- The data is fully anonymised and aggregated before use

We use anonymised, aggregated, and synthetic data to improve our models.

### 5.4 Right to explanation

Candidates who receive an AI-generated screening verdict may request a plain-English explanation of the reasoning. Employers can make this available through the Platform or candidates may request it at privacy@hrops.io.

## 10. Data Security

HR Ops maintains a formal Information Security Management System aligned to **ISO/IEC 27001** and validated through our **SOC 2 Type II** certification. Key controls include:

- **Encryption at rest:** AES-256 for all data stored on HR Ops infrastructure
- **Encryption in transit:** TLS 1.2 minimum, TLS 1.3 preferred, for all data in transit
- **Access control:** Role-based access, principle of least privilege, MFA mandatory for all HR Ops employees
- **Penetration testing:** Annual third-party penetration testing
- **Vulnerability management:** Continuous scanning; critical vulnerabilities patched within 72 hours
- **Background checks:** All HR Ops employees with access to customer data undergo background verification
- **Incident response:** Documented IR plan; data breach notification to affected customers within 72 hours of discovery; DPDP/GDPR authorities notified as required by law

Our current SOC 2 Type II report and ISO 27001 certificate are available to Enterprise customers under NDA. Contact security@hrops.io.
