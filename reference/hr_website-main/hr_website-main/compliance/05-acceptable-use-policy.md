## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") defines the permitted and prohibited uses of the HR Ops platform ("**Platform**"). It applies to all customers, authorised users, agency partners, and any person who accesses the Platform.

This AUP forms part of the Terms of Service. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given to them in the Terms of Service.

Violating this AUP may result in immediate suspension or permanent termination of your account, without refund, and may expose you to legal liability.

## 2. Permitted Uses

You may use the Platform to:

- Post genuine job openings and manage genuine recruitment pipelines for roles within your organisation or your clients' organisations (for Agency Customers)
- Collect and evaluate applications from candidates who have been informed that their data will be processed using HR Ops
- Use AI screening features to assist â€” not replace â€” human hiring decisions
- Send Magic Link assessments to candidates who have been invited to apply
- Manage commission records, placements, and agency-client relationships
- Generate and share analytics reports about your own hiring data
- Integrate the Platform with authorised third-party tools via the HR Ops API, within your plan's API limits
- Configure and share white-label client portals with your employer clients (Agency Pro and Enterprise plans only)

## 3. Prohibited Uses

### 3.1 Illegal and harmful conduct

You must not use the Platform to:

- Violate any applicable law, regulation, or court order â€” including employment law, data protection law, consumer protection law, and anti-discrimination law
- Discriminate against candidates on the basis of any protected characteristic including caste, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, pregnancy, marital status, disability, age, nationality, or political opinion, in violation of Indian law (Constitution of India Articles 14â€“16; Equal Remuneration Act 1976; Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016; Maternity Benefit Act 1961) or any other applicable law
- Post fraudulent, misleading, or non-existent job listings
- Collect personal data from candidates without a lawful basis and without informing them of the processing
- Process personal data for purposes incompatible with the hiring process for which it was collected
- Process personal data of individuals without their knowledge for profiling, surveillance, or any non-hiring purpose
- Engage in any form of human trafficking, forced labour recruitment, or deceptive migration recruitment

### 3.2 Platform integrity

You must not:

- Attempt to gain unauthorised access to any part of the Platform, other customer accounts, or HR Ops infrastructure
- Reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to derive the source code or AI models underlying the Platform
- Introduce malware, viruses, worms, Trojan horses, logic bombs, or any other malicious code
- Conduct penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, or load testing of the Platform without prior written authorisation from HR Ops (security@hrops.io)
- Interfere with, disrupt, or degrade the performance or availability of the Platform for other users
- Use automated scripts, bots, or scrapers to access the Platform in ways not permitted by the official API
- Circumvent, disable, or interfere with any security feature, access control, or rate limit
- Attempt to de-anonymise, re-identify, or link anonymised or aggregated data to specific individuals
- Access or attempt to access data belonging to other customers

### 3.3 Content standards

You must not upload, post, or transmit through the Platform any content that:

- Is false, misleading, or fraudulent
- Infringes any third party's intellectual property, privacy, or other rights
- Is defamatory, harassing, abusive, threatening, or discriminatory
- Contains personal data of individuals who have not consented to being processed for the relevant purpose
- Constitutes unsolicited commercial communications (spam) sent to candidates
- Violates any applicable content law, including the Information Technology Act 2000 (India) and the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021

### 3.4 AI feature misuse

You must not use AI features to:

- Make final, binding hiring decisions without human review â€” AI verdicts are recommendations only
- Screen or exclude candidates on the basis of characteristics that are irrelevant to role fitness or that constitute unlawful discrimination
- Generate misleading or fabricated candidate profiles or assessment results
- Probe or attempt to extract the underlying AI model's training data, weights, or system prompts
- Submit synthetic, fabricated, or AI-generated resumes to test or game the screening system without prior written consent from HR Ops

### 3.5 Resale and sublicensing

You must not:

- Resell, sublicense, or provide Platform access to third parties not covered by your plan
- White-label or rebrand the Platform as your own product (except Agency Pro/Enterprise white-label features, which are expressly permitted within their intended scope)
- Share login credentials across multiple individuals
- Create multiple accounts to circumvent plan limits or to obtain free trial benefits more than once

### 3.6 Data scraping and extraction

You must not:

- Systematically download, copy, or extract candidate data for use outside the Platform in ways not permitted by your plan
- Use Platform data to build a competing product or service
- Share candidate data submitted through HR Ops with other competing hiring platforms without candidate consent

## 4. Candidate Data â€” Special Obligations

Because HR Ops processes personal data about candidates on your behalf, you have specific obligations:

### 4.1 Before collecting candidate data

- Publish a clear privacy notice informing candidates that HR Ops processes their data, what data is collected, the purpose, and their rights
- Obtain any consent required by applicable law before collecting sensitive information
- Provide candidates with HR Ops's candidate-facing privacy notice (available in Platform Settings > Candidate Privacy Notice)

### 4.2 During the hiring process

- Only collect candidate data that is relevant and necessary for the role
- Not process candidate data for purposes other than the specific hiring process for which it was collected
- Not share candidate data across separate employers or agency clients without candidate consent
- Inform candidates if they will be evaluated using automated AI screening tools

### 4.3 After the hiring process

- Delete or anonymise candidate data within the retention period you have configured in Platform settings (maximum 24 months after process close for unsuccessful candidates, unless a longer period is required by law)
- Do not retain candidate data for speculative future hiring without candidates' explicit consent to this purpose
- Respond to candidate access, correction, and deletion requests promptly (HR Ops provides tools to assist â€” see Settings > Data Management)

## 5. Agency-Specific Obligations

Agency Customers using HR Ops on behalf of employer clients have additional obligations:

- Maintain valid, written agency agreements with each employer client before managing their recruitment through HR Ops
- Only upload candidate data to an employer client's workspace with that client's authorisation
- Not cross-contaminate candidate data â€” do not share a candidate submitted to one employer with a different employer client without the candidate's explicit consent
- Accurately record and not falsify commission calculations, placement dates, or client approval records
- Ensure employer clients are informed that HR Ops is the platform used and that data is processed in accordance with HR Ops's Privacy Policy and DPA
- Not use white-label features to suggest the platform is entirely proprietary software built by the agency, in a way that deceives employer clients about the nature of data processing
