### 6.1 AI verdicts are recommendations

The AI screening feature produces Proceed / Maybe / Decline verdicts with associated reasoning. These are **decision-support tools**, not final decisions. You acknowledge that:
- All hiring decisions must involve a human decision-maker
- HR Ops AI verdicts are one input among many — not a substitute for interview, assessment, or human judgment
- You are solely responsible for all hiring decisions made using the Platform

### 6.2 AI accuracy

We make no warranty that AI screening verdicts are accurate, complete, or free from bias. We publish our model evaluation methodology and bias testing results at hrops.io/ai-transparency. You should review this before relying on AI features.

### 6.3 EU AI Act compliance

Recruitment AI is classified as a **high-risk AI system** under the EU AI Act. For customers using HR Ops to evaluate candidates who are EU residents:
- HR Ops provides the technical documentation required under EU AI Act Article 11
- Candidates must be informed that automated tools are used in evaluation (see your obligations under Clause 8.3)
- Human oversight mechanisms are built into the Platform and must be used

### 6.4 Prohibited AI uses

You may not use HR Ops AI features to:
- Make final, irreversible hiring decisions without human review
- Screen candidates on the basis of protected characteristics (caste, religion, sex, disability, pregnancy, political opinion, etc.)
- Create profiles of individuals not related to the hiring process for which they applied
- Infer sensitive attributes not relevant to role fitness
